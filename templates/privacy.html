Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

PatchPilot ("we", "us", "our") is owned and operated by Jayam Shah. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. What we collect

We collect your email address and a hashed password when you create an account. We store the GitHub repository URLs you register. We store the API keys we generate for you. We store logs of pipeline activity (changelog checks, patches generated, PRs opened).

2. What we do NOT collect

We do not store your source code permanently. We do not sell your data to any third party. We do not track your browsing behavior. We do not use cookies beyond what is necessary to operate the Service.

3. GitHub access

When you register a repository, PatchPilot uses your GitHub Personal Access Token to read your code and open pull requests. We use this token only for the purpose of generating patches. We do not share this token with any third party.

4. Email

We send email notifications when a pull request is opened on your behalf. We use Resend to deliver these emails. We do not send marketing emails without your consent.

5. Data storage

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Render (Oregon, US). We take reasonable measures to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing jayamshah04@gmail.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of significant changes via email.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email jayamshah04@gmail.com
